## Normalization to use throughout

Write

- `L=log D`, `log P=L^9`, `alpha=π L^-9`, `log T=L^(11/10)`
- `d=L′(1,chi)>0`, `theta=phi(D)/D`
- `q_D=prod_(q|D) q/(q+1)` and `a=(6/π²)d²q_D`
- `M=𝒫`, the actual prime mass; normalize actual mean errors by `a M`
- `B(L)=1+9 log L`; `polylog(L)` always denotes a fixed explicitly source-bounded power, not an unspecified power of L

The original fixed-profile S_j has leading scale `a alpha`; after Proposition 7.1 its coefficient is multiplied by `q_j/alpha` and by M. Thus a raw S_j error `E_S` enters the normalized energy at scale `E_S/(a alpha)`. To resolve a proposed normalized correction `epsilon_D=L^-8`, a sufficient raw S_j error is `o(a alpha L^-8)=o(a L^-17)`. A projected combination of errors can satisfy this even when its individual summands do not, but the cancellation needs proof.

The new `lemma171_actual_main_gt_half` supplies eventual `a>1/2` under (A), using the actual n=1 term of the original 17.1 sum. Its frozen packet's verification records four public declarations, six regressions, source/dependency hashes, and only the standard three axioms. This legitimizes conversion of absolute `o(M)` to relative `o(a M)`; it supplies no additional log-power saving.

There is a stronger normalization identity useful for derivative-weighted errors:

\[
 q_D\ge\theta,\qquad d^2/a=(\pi^2/6)q_D^{-1}\le(\pi^2/6)\theta^{-1}.
\]

The primewise inequality is `q/(q+1) >= 1-1/q`. `lemma84_reciprocal_totient_uniform` in `Lemma84WeightedArithmetic.lean`, applied to n=D, y=L, yields

\[
 \theta^{-1}\le e^{2/\log 2}(1+\log L)^6.
\]

Consequently `alpha theta^-2 = O(L^-9(1+log L)^12)=o(L^-8)`. These are mathematical consequences of the actual definitions and proved local bounds; a downstream Lean capstone stating this exact combined normalization has not been claimed. Do not instead replace d² by `O(L^4)` or d³ by `O(L^6)` before dividing by a.

## Fixed inner support: what it does and does not solve

Fix a concrete smooth real cutoff: put `eta(x)=exp(-1/x)` for x>0 and `eta(x)=0` for x<=0, and set `chi(t)=eta(.503-t)/(eta(.503-t)+eta(t-.501))`. Its denominator never vanishes, chi=1 on `[0,.501]`, and chi=0 on `[.503,1]`. For each glued null mode `h_j=e^(-iπjt)`, set

\[
 \phi_j=\chi h_j,\quad q_j=(1-\chi)h_j,\quad
 \psi_j(t)=\overline{q_j(1-t)}.
\]

Then H1 support is at most `.503`, H2 at most `.499`. Eventually `10 L^-7.9<.001`, so both lie strictly inside the original supports. This correctly avoids applying a moving-P2 endpoint correction to a profile that is already zero near it. Since chi is smooth and constant on either side, these profiles can vanish to every order at their support endpoints. Fix chi explicitly before computation or any constants/quantifiers in a new theorem.

However, the current `Lemma84Section8Objects.lean` defines its hybrid replacement by `dr<P_mu/T`; its boundary is `P_mu/T<=dr<P_mu`, equivalently `1<=x=P_mu/(dr)<=T`. Replacing P_mu by a smaller actual cutoff Q would simply produce a new layer `Q/T<=dr<Q`. No existing theorem says this layer disappears when the profile is inset. The existing smoothing bridge hardcodes the two source ramps and their logarithmic denominators. It cannot be applied to a smooth envelope by relabeling Q or setting the old boundary term to zero.

Endpoint flatness is a legitimate reason to investigate a **new** weighted ξ estimate. On a logarithmic endpoint layer of width `delta_T=L^-7.9`, a profile vanishing to order m has size O(delta_T^m), its derivative O(delta_T^(m−1)), and its Volterra tail O(delta_T^(m+1)), with fixed derivative constants. Thus the model F/G tails exhibit possible extra powers. But this observation is not yet an arithmetic bound for the actual ξ sum, its main term, and all mixed products. Nor does endpoint flatness alone improve interior replacement or the exceptional-family bound. An Abel/superposition proof introduces intermediate cutoff layers that also must be accounted for.

The source-supported next possibility for this boundary is therefore to reprove the needed weighted ξ bridge for this fixed family (or prove its combined projected error directly), retaining the true arithmetic weights and uniform derivative/profile constants. It is not justified to claim that the existing ramp boundary theorem has already been bypassed. No numerical search is needed to establish this distinction.

## Bounded next step and stopping rule

1. **Pick the exact scope:** the three-dimensional span of the displayed fixed decompositions, with coefficients `z∈C³`, `||z||₂=1`. This is one chosen subspace of the leading pair-space nullspace, not the whole nullspace. The direct-negative-N route is the smallest target; adding J reintroduces the mixed/R/transfer obligations.
2. **Define the actual object:** the source weighted square mean of `H1_z+Z conj(H2_z)`, normalized by the actual `a M`. Keep the original shifts, conductor and cutoff conventions. Prove that these coefficient sequences satisfy the actual P7/P14 support and coefficient hypotheses. Use both profile norms, including the smooth cutoff's derivatives; the glued norm alone is insufficient.
3. **Prove a projected source identity before a sign search:** retain exact β/P4 residues, the true Section 15 residue decomposition, corrected Section 16 two-pole expressions, and actual arithmetic/tail terms. Identify all projected contributions with bounds ≥L^-8; prove cancellation where available. A direct estimate for each relevant `z*E_D z` or the projected 3×3 remainder is sufficient. Uniform entrywise `o(L^-8)` for the *whole* infinite profile space is unnecessary.
4. **Quantitative goal:** if a nonzero projected coefficient is truly first at scale ε_D, prove a uniform remainder `o(ε_D)` on this fixed span, or a finite explicit error smaller than a certified strict margin. At ε_D=L^-8, Section 8 needs a raw projected bound `o(a L^-17)`; the Ψ₂ contribution needs `o(a M L^-8)`. Current generic estimates fail both requirements. The easier exact `a/theta/d` normalization bridge can be completed independently, but it is not the missing signed main argument.
5. **Only then compute/certify sign.** A certified negative direction would contradict original (2.16), conditional on the completed arithmetic identity. A PSD projected correction stops this chosen family at that resolved order only. If the first coefficient cancels, identify the next order and redo the precision budget; do not assume L^-8 is first or final.

If one instead studies a near-equality augmented Gram matrix involving J and a positive complement, control the normalized coefficient vector and the relevant Schur complement. A coupling to the complement that is larger than L^-8 can contribute through its square after optimization. Coercivity of the limiting glued complement does not control the overlap-cancellation directions, and no whole-pair exclusion follows from a three-dimensional projected test.

**Smallest feasible closed-form bridge identified:** formalize the source-specific finite-prefix τ4 exceptional estimate in `PREFIX_TAU4_BRIDGE.md`, preserving the accepted global τ5 infinite-tail bounds. Its hypotheses match the actual convolution structure and the existing bounded b coefficients; it repairs one currently insufficient error budget without changing the prime window or (A). The weighted ξ boundary and projected actual mean remain separate, more substantial work.

**Present stopping condition:** the required actual generalized/projected interface and error precision have not yet been proved. Stop the claim of a finite-D repair at that point. Continue the source-identification work and the specific projected/weighted-error bridge, rather than interpreting a floating-point eigenvalue or a coarse exported power as mathematical evidence for or against the original theorem.
