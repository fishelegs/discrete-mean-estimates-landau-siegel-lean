# Explicit correction record

The initial draft overstated what had been established for C₁: it suggested that completion from the good family Ψ₁ to the full primitive family had already been paid. That claim is retracted. The [independent review](INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) identified the gap, and the [author's revision record](REVIEW_REVISIONS.md) records its removal before the accepted revised derivation was finalized.

This record preserves the correction rather than presenting the initial claim as valid. The initial pre-review draft is not included. Its overclaim is described from the surviving revision log and independent review; no unavailable verbatim sentence has been reconstructed.

The accepted correction has four substantive parts:

1. The O(ML⁻⁴⁵) extension is for H(GA,FB), whose residue integrand has one inverse Z factor. It is not a bound for C₁ or T₁.
2. C₁'s right-side extra inverse Zχψ creates the two-factor gamma scale Dp²t₀². The H truncation at m<P² cannot discard the relevant long saddle blocks. Separate right tails and exceptional-family completion remain required.
3. C₁'s transformed left side has a different coefficient arrangement and length geometry. Its moments, family completion, and tails require a separate argument.
4. The summed contour shifts, horizontal pieces, and crossed-pole ledger must be justified. Exact functional equations and finite-character identities alone do not establish an actual-zero-mean formula.

The congruence kernel on the right and Kl₂(±ℓ/(mn);p) kernel on the transformed left are retained as exact arithmetic identities within a conditional analytic reduction. The mn≈Dℓ localization is only the formal constant-multiplier comparison until a summed replacement estimate is proved. T₁'s distinct single-Gauss kernel similarly does not settle its actual mean or target-transfer error.

Other review corrections are preserved in the revision log: truncate before shifting the exceptional-family finite polynomial; specify the inherited exact gamma branch on the full vertical line; require coefficient sequences independent of p and ψ with fixed bounds; and retain energy factors when discussing the compensated near-null construction.

The next stopping condition is an actual evaluation of the remaining joint means, a complete error ledger, and a signed target/residual calculation at the required precision. This bundle asserts none of those later conclusions.
