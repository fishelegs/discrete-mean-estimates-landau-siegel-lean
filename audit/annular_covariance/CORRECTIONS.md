# Corrections and scope boundaries

These points describe the accepted argument; they are not edits to the frozen dyadic mathematics.

1. C1 uses different tail cutoffs: right P^0.999, left P^1.003 in the dyadic report. The right cutoff is invalid for the left tail.
2. Both T1 sides need P^1.005 for the literal original target support. The dual-left endpoint P^1.003 has zero power gap and does not absorb the height factor.
3. Hard truncation is controlled by the positive convolution |kappa|*|kappa|, not a potentially canceling full complex convolution.
4. The actual small-shift relation beta3=beta1+beta2 gives the harmonic costs L^36 and L^144. A coarse d4-only moment does not give this completion budget.
5. Both contour sides are oriented upward, hence right minus left. Exact inherited gamma branches, all D and p factors, unit restrictions, and positive even-principal corrections for the one/three-Gauss T1 sums are retained.
6. A good-family residue attachment precedes family completion. Only finite polynomials are moved to the critical line for bad-character estimates; their reciprocal L-functions are not moved through zeros.
7. The finite-window representation error is O(a^-1 L^-14), with C1 right separately O(a^-1 L^-200). This is an unevaluated arithmetic representation, not a favorable-sign asymptotic.
8. A widened fixed-log support class, if included, is a separate addendum. It cannot be inferred by silently widening the frozen dyadic supports.
9. Coefficient harmonic mass and logarithmic profile mass do not establish actual mu-norm or a nondegenerate mu-Gram matrix. Residual normalization remains unproved; dividing by a vanishing residual can amplify the O(L^-14) entry errors.
