## Admissible shifts

**Accepted within the complete leading source-residue model:** for

    0 < θ₁ ≤ 1,
    k ≤ θ₂ < θ₃ = θ₁+θ₂ ≤ k+1,
    k ∈ Z, k ≥ 1, θ₂ > θ₁,

the glued form N is positive semidefinite. Put a=πθ₁, b=πθ₂, h=φ+conj(ψ(1−·)). The exact periodic completion gives

    N(h) = 2C Σ(n∈Z) (nπ)(nπ−a)(nπ−b)(nπ−a−b)|uₙ|² ≥ 0,
    C > 0.

Here uₙ are the length-two periodic H² primitive's coefficients in exp(−inπt). The model theorem holds for every h∈H¹[0,1]; its source decomposition uses H¹ profiles with φ vanishing near 1 and ψ supported strictly below 1/2, with continuous vanishing at its support edge. Endpoint values at zero are unrestricted. Arithmetic attachments would use fixed C³² profiles and D-independent coefficient bounds; H¹ density gives no uniform arithmetic remainder for growing families.

For (θ₁,θ₂,θ₃)=(1/2,9/4,11/4),

    N(h) ≥ π(77+2√2)/112 · ||h||²₂ > 2||h||²₂  for h≠0.

The actual leading source target is matched to this same form: Lw=BN(h,J), Rw=N(J)>0, hence |Lw|²≤RwN. At (1,k,k+1), k≥2, the glued kernel consists of the three integer Fourier modes. The admissible inward beta variation is positive on that kernel. This is a beta-only variation, with no conclusion about the complete finite-D correction.

Boundary points require inward perturbations using the same compatible gap constant. Individual shift sizes for the requested k=3,4 cases fit the local source radius; arbitrary k≥5 is only an algebraic claim here. The derived new complex source weights do not generalize the existing original-integer-shift Lean ports. Altered-shift same-side, high-tail, low-term, exceptional-family, contour, and target-transfer attachments remain unproved.

The fixed constant leading-margin scan therefore stops in this class. Lower-order boundary effects, overlap-cancellation pairs h=0, other interval geometries, and other arithmetic constructions remain outside that conclusion. ## Phase arithmetic

**Accepted bounded facts:** the exact conductor pairing normalizes to χ(p)EH, so only explicit χ(p) compensation gives EH. For even χ this compensated trial equals H; for odd χ it is exponentially close at positive height. The uncompensated prime-sign weighted energy remains unproved. A central-value ramified-deletion error is O(qD⁻¹d₈(D)(1+log X)¹²(1+log(2Q))⁴), giving O(D⁻¹ᐟ²L⁴⁸) after division by q at q=P for all real primitive conductors. This settles one deletion step only. The verified MQW theorem gives a bare two-polynomial root-average bound Oε(p⁻³ᐟ²⁰⁰⁺ε), up to logarithms.

The kernels must remain distinct: C₀ has Kl₂, the proposed rψ-twisted C₁ has congruence conditions, and its target T₁ has a single-Gauss kernel. Their κβ convolution, gamma Mellin kernel, selected-family passage, zero weights, residue conversion, and complete target/transfer entries still need joint estimates. No favorable signed residual or full ratio is certified. An L⁻⁸ proposal needs the complete correction and joint normalized errors o(L⁻⁸).
