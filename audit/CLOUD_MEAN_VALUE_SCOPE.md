## What the validation does and does not certify

All production proof/definition bytes and original standalone regression bytes
are preserved. Public declarations are deduplicated by exact module bytes,
compared to the frozen manifests, and individually checked for axioms confined
to propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound. No sorry/admit/custom axiom is
accepted by the source/audit checks.

Published dependencies are read-only inputs pinned by source and olean hashes;
they are not independently re-proved by this union run. Historical manifests
and source audits are preserved as provenance. This refresh does not claim a
full-paper proof, a numerical effectivity certificate, either global target,
or (7.11).
