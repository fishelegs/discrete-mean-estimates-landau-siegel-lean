# Exact finite Möbius identity: semantic review

The central review compared every definition and endpoint with the finite identity in the accepted multilinear source proof. The coefficient ring is arbitrary and commutative; the regression module separately instantiates the actual complex coefficients. ArithmeticFunction multiplication is Dirichlet convolution. Its unit is delta, while zeta is one at positive indices and zero at zero. The truncation is literally the inclusive condition n <= U, and its canonical integer cast is proved.

The final identity has no inverse hypothesis: it uses Mathlib's proved Möbius-zeta inverse. Its generic polynomial helper has that hypothesis and is correctly instantiated. Positive J and n <= U^J are exactly the finite range. The endpoint n=U^J and n=1 are separately checked. U=0 and n=0 follow the library's zero convention; intended analytic uses take U>=1. The j=0,...,J-1 indexing gives the exact conventional coefficient vector (4,-6,4,-1) at J=4.

The support proof first vanishes on n<=U, then on n<=U^J, then after convolution by Möbius. It uses actual divisor pairs. It neither assumes the final finite identity nor expands an infinite inverse series on the critical line. The namespace qualification is the only source change in central integration.

This proves the finite arithmetic identity. It does not prove the analytic completion, the square-factor weighted-tail estimate, the remaining weak-pair covariance, or a strict signed half-norm gain. No original numbered theorem is added to the completed count.
