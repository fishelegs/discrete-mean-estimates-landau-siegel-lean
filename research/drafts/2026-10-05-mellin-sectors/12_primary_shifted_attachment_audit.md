# Primary theorem attachment audit for the actual shifted correlations

Draft research note dated 2026-10-05. Independently source-reviewed as a bounded audit of six specified printed theorem interfaces and their direct attachment budgets, with the essential positive-component clarification integrated into Section 5. These tested direct routes do not establish the required actual shifted-coefficient remainder. This is not a universal impossibility claim, an assertion that the actual contributions are large, or a criticism of the source theorems. Adapted mixed-character, mollified, high-height or aggregate arguments remain possible and unproved.

Section 5's physical difference r=ell-k, divisibility p|r, exceptional-set counting and good-error aggregation apply ONLY to the positive congruence ell=k mod p. The reflected component instead has p|(k+ell); its arithmetic interface remains separately unpaid. No arithmetic density, remainder estimate, balanced-energy gain, final strict gap or new Lean certificate is supplied here.

All source papers are cited by links to their public originals. The mathematical audit below is original work; publisher PDFs and full text are not included in this collection. The original assumption and target, finite inverse, all masks and both congruence signs are unchanged.

## 1. The actual interface that must be met

We retain the accepted Gaussian target and carrier interface. The coefficient measures are the exact A(k)conjugate(B(ell))/sqrt(k ell) correlations, with d,e<=D^4 retained, the actual nu_beta=(n^-beta_1)*chi and d23=(n^-beta_2)*(n^-beta_3), all transported complex Mellin exponents, both parity signs, all p-unit masks, and p in the ORIGINAL interval P<p<P(1+L^-68).

Here L=log D, B=log P=L^9, t_c=2pi L^519, W=L^400, delta=L^-395 and all retained Mellin heights have size at most V=L^6. The actual shifted equations are

    positive: ell=k+r, r=h p not equal 0;
    negative: ell=h p-k>0,

with |log(ell/k)|<=delta. The time kernel retains exp(i t_c log(ell/k)); its proved variation is O(L^119). One auditable sufficient arithmetic input is the accepted weighted cumulative remainder bound

    sum_(ij,p,a)(p-1)/2 integral_(ten-height box)
         |Omega| sup_theta |E([-delta,theta])| d lambda/(2pi)^10
       <<P^2 L^(b-119), fixed b<64.               (1.1)

It remains unproved. No theorem in this audit is silently assumed to furnish a density or E.

Write

    T0=X Q_nu Q,
    log T0=2B+(9/2)L+1038log L+O(1),
    T0 asymp P^2 D^(9/2)L^1038.                  (1.2)

This is a natural weighted output scale, not a hard cutoff or a proved lower bound on the actual mass there. The safe post-AFE finite support is larger, k,ell<=D^4P^6. No accepted estimate has deleted every dyadic sector near (1.2).

At a dyadic output size x comparable to (1.2), the LITERAL near band permits physical differences |r| of order x L^-395. The narrower effective Gaussian scale is x/W=x L^-400, five log powers smaller. Testing a black-box bound on this smaller scale is favorable to that bound; it does not delete the rest of the retained near band.

Each coefficient has four underlying shifted Dirichlet factors, one carrying chi, multiplied by a finite inverse polynomial and finite input restrictions. They are not ordinary d_2 coefficients. If d,e are opened, the arithmetic relation is e v-d u=h p, with unequal slopes up to D^4; it does not become v-u=h by renaming. Full infinite-inverse cancellation at beta_1=0 would change this object and is not available at the actual critical heights.

## 2. CLMR's unconditional eighth moment and its direct high-height budget

Primary: Chandee, Li, Matomaki and Radziwill, arXiv:2307.13194v1. Theorem 1.1 is on PDF page 2; Proposition 3.2 on PDF page 5. The theorem gives the unconditional completed eighth moment over primitive even characters and a fixed smooth all-moduli average, with the gamma-weighted t integral centered at zero. The proposition gives, for Q,T>=3 and 0<=c<=1/100,

    sum_(q<=Q)sum_(chi mod q) integral_0^T
       |L(1/2+c+it,chi)|^8 dt
       <<Q^2 T^2(log(QT))^16.                    (2.1)

Odd parity is covered by the proposition. This is the 2023 unconditional result, not the earlier GRH-conditional eighth moment. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13194v1)

Here are our explicit attachment calculations. On the original interval, and after any retained Mellin/shift displacement, the L-function heights lie in [0,2t_c] eventually. The normalized Gaussian density is O(1/W). Enlarging a NONNEGATIVE pure eighth moment to all moduli q<=2P is legitimate and yields

    M_psi<<P^2 (t_c^2/W) B^16
          <<P^2 L^(1038-400+144)=P^2 L^782.      (2.2)

There is no permission to replace this complete all-moduli moment bound by a sparse-prime count. For the chi psi factor, the character has conductor Dp and embeds injectively in the all-character family q<=2DP. The same argument gives M_chipsi<<D^2P^2L^782.

The original degree-four product has THREE psi factors and ONE chi psi factor. Holder on the actual family/time measure gives the product-square bound

    (M_psi^3 M_chipsi)^(1/4)<<D^(1/2)P^2L^782.

The literal finite G has the coarse uniform bound

    |G_z(u_1)|<=2sum_(d<=D^4)tau_2(d)/sqrt(d)<<D^2L.

Thus the direct supremum/Holder attachment retains

    P^2 D^(9/2)L^784                            (2.3)

before the outer Mellin norm cost. The three original Gaussian Mellin kernels contribute (log L)^6 in squared norm, giving the valid but inadequate bound O(P^2D^(9/2)L^784(log L)^6). Heights outside the retained box are paid by the already established absolute tails, or directly by periodic-character polynomial bounds against their Gaussian decay. All original labels and finite G can therefore be retained in this failed-budget test. We claim no improvement over prior coarse bounds.

The completed-moment theorem itself cannot be translated to t_c for free. Stirling gives

    |Gamma(1/4+it/2)|^8 asymp |t|^-2 exp(-2pi|t|)

at large real t. Domination by that theorem's weight near t_c would introduce an exponential exp(2pi(t_c+H)), rather than the original normalized Gaussian. Replacing t by t+t_c changes the L-functions and their gamma weight; no such translated mixed/mollified theorem is printed there. Expanding G termwise instead would require uniformly controlled twisted moments, not the untwisted theorem. These are failures of the stated interface, not limitations proved for an adapted argument.

## 3. A complementary-divisor contraction is also not automatic

In the CLMR proof sketch, PDF pages 3-4, the pure coefficient length is first reduced to N=Q^2/exp((log Q)^epsilon_0). The complementary divisor then has a smaller hybrid conductor, and the dual length contracts. This is a structural motivation in that paper, not a theorem about our coefficients. [Primary proof sketch](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13194v1)

Our retained weighted scale T0 exceeds P^2 by D^(9/2)L^1038. On the narrower effective difference scale |r| about T0/W, the complementary h=r/p can be of size

    h about P D^(9/2)L^638,
    h t_c about P D^(9/2)L^1157.                 (3.1)

The ACTUAL carrier height is t_c, not merely its Gaussian bandwidth W. Even the optimistic pure-degree-four dual-length comparison would give (h t_c)^2/T0 about D^(9/2)L^1276, which expands rather than contracts. The mixed character and finite inverse have not been removed by that comparison. Equation (3.1) asserts allowed scales, not nonzero mass on them. A new argument could reduce the relevant ranges, but the printed contraction cannot simply be imported before that reduction is proved.

## 4. MRT's higher divisor correlations and the physical shift variable

Primary I: Matomaki, Radziwill and Tao, arXiv:1707.01315v3, Theorem 1.3(ii), PDF page 8. It treats ordinary d_k(n)d_l(n+r), fixed k,l, for almost all integer r in |r-r_0|<=H, with x^(8/33+epsilon)<=H<=x^(1-epsilon), 0<=r_0<=x^(1-epsilon). Its displayed error is O(x(log x)^(-A)), with O(H(log x)^(-A)) exceptions for each fixed A. [Primary theorem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.01315v3)

Primary II: arXiv:1712.08840v2, Theorem 1.1, PDF page 2. It lowers H to a fixed logarithmic power, still with H<=x^(1-epsilon), and gives an average absolute error o(Hx(log x)^(k+l-2)) for the leading term. It does not supply a specified power saving. [Primary theorem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.08840v2)

The coefficient, conductor and range gates are separate. The actual coefficients are not the ordinary d_k,d_l in those statements: they retain chi of growing conductor D, complex Mellin heights up to L^6, finite inverse factors and unequal slopes after opening d,e. No uniform extension in those parameters is supplied by the printed theorems. Also the physical shift is r=hp, not the smaller complementary index h. At x about T0,

    (x/W)/x^(1-epsilon)=x^epsilon/L^400 ->infinity

for every FIXED epsilon>0; the literal band is wider still. Substituting h=r/p into the theorem's shift variable would change the sequence to B(k+p h), which is an unproved progression-restricted variant. Difference-correlation clauses also do not automatically supply our negative reflected piece ell=hp-k.

These mismatches already prevent invocation as stated. The following favorable ordinary-divisor budget test shows why the exceptional-set issue must be addressed even in ranges where the theorem DOES apply.

## 5. Aggregate exceptional and good-error budgets over the actual prime labels

Scope clarification (positive difference component only):

Every use in that section of the physical shift r=ell-k, the condition p dividing r, the at-most-two-large-primes bound, and the aggregate exceptional or good-error budgets applies ONLY to the POSITIVE difference-congruence component ell congruent to k modulo p. MRT's inspected divisor-correlation statements are difference-shift statements, which is the component tested by that calculation.

For the REFLECTED component ell congruent to -k modulo p, the divisibility condition is p dividing k+ell, not p dividing ell-k. One must not import the same exceptional-difference-set accounting into that component. A size bound on prime divisors of k+ell would concern a different sum variable and does not turn an exceptional set for difference shifts into a theorem for reflected correlations. The required reflected arithmetic interface remains separately unproved, as already stated in Sections 4 and 8 of the report.

The failure of the stated positive-component route to imply the required weighted remainder is enough to reject the proposed complete direct attachment. It does not show that the actual positive exceptional contribution is large, nor that an adapted averaged argument or a separate reflected estimate is impossible. This addendum changes no exponent or source theorem statement.

For nonzero physical r with |r|<P^3, at most TWO distinct primes p>P divide r. Hence

    sum_(p in original window,p divides r)p<<P.  (5.1)

At x about T0 and |r|<=2delta x, this condition holds eventually because x=P^(2+o(1)). This saves a full prime-family factor relative to a naive double count; it must not be ignored.

The actual transported coefficients obey |A(k)|,|B(k)|<=2tau_6(k) on the retained contours. Thus on comparable dyadic k,ell about x, Cauchy and tau_6^2<=tau_36 give the crude harmonic bound per physical shift

    sum_k |A(k)B(k+r)|/sqrt(k(k+r))
       <<(log x)^35.                            (5.2)

This is a possible available upper bound, not a lower bound for an actual exceptional contribution.

Suppose optimistically that an almost-all theorem's exceptional budget could be used for the actual coefficients and relevant partial sums, even granting one common exceptional set across the prime labels. If that set E has size H(log x)^(-A), then (5.1)-(5.2) yield only the aggregate budget

    P H(log x)^(35-A),                           (5.3)

before the explicit Mellin and kernel-variation factors in the accepted criterion. Fixed powers of log x cannot remove a positive P-power excess.

This test need not rely on extending the theorem's large-shift range: choose its admissible subrange H=x^(2/3), with fixed sufficiently small epsilon. It lies inside our literal near band eventually, but (5.3) has size

    P x^(2/3)(log x)^(35-A)
       about P^(7/3)D^3 L^692(log x)^(35-A).      (5.4)

It therefore does not imply a P^2 times fixed-logarithm remainder budget by absolute aggregation. A single-prime exceptional-set example alone would not establish this aggregate point; the large-prime-divisor accounting (5.1) is explicitly included.

Even granting a range extension to the narrower effective Gaussian H=x/W, the analogous available bound is P^3D^(9/2)L^638 times fixed log powers. This is a FAVORABLE test on a smaller subband, not an unproved deletion of the outer near region.

For the nonexceptional shifts, a normalized O((log x)^(-A)) error per shift, summed absolutely over selected multiples of p, gives at best a bound of order

    P H L^-68(log x)^(-A)

using the literal window count. At H=x^(2/3) this also retains P^(7/3). The weaker short-range theorem does not improve that issue. These calculations show what the printed log-saving error information FAILS TO IMPLY by the stated route. They do not prove actual exceptional or good-error contributions are large. Extra averaged-p cancellation, the original carrier oscillation, or a stronger uniform arithmetic theorem could change the conclusion.

## 6. The d_k times d_2 candidates have a different degree and shift scope

Topacogullari, arXiv:1605.02364v1, Theorems 1.1-1.2, PDF page 2, treats d_k against d_2, k>=4. The displayed clean range is |r|<<x^(15/19); the text discusses extension to x^(1-epsilon) with changed errors/main terms. [Primary theorem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1605.02364v1)

Lau's current author preprint arXiv:2509.07556v2, dated 9 July 2026, Theorem 1.1, PDF page 2, also treats smoothed d_k against d_2. It allows |r|<<x^(25/28-eta) and displays errors x^(1-7eta/128+epsilon)+x^(127/128+epsilon). The more detailed Theorem 1.2 on PDF page 3 assumes |r|<<x^(1-epsilon). [Primary current version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.07556v2)

Neither printed interface is a four-factor against four-factor mixed-character correlation with the finite inverse left in place. The physical shifts x/W and x delta exceed the stated fixed-power ranges at x about T0. Their smooth weights are fixed theorem inputs; a uniform dependence on the actual complex Mellin shifts and conductor D would also have to be established. Applying a positive divisor majorant cannot recover a signed shifted-correlation main term.

For scale only, if one hypothetically had a uniform per-physical-shift error O(x^alpha) and summed it absolutely with harmonic normalization over H about x/W shifts selected by p, the aggregate costs P H x^(alpha-1), i.e. P x^alpha/W. Since x=P^(2+o(1)), reaching P^2 by this route requires alpha<=1/2 up to explicitly paid subpower factors. Even alpha=1/2 leaves D^(9/4)L^119 in this optimistic ledger, so a square-root error in x alone would still need its conductor/finite-inverse loss paid. A small unspecified power saving below x, by itself, is not enough. This is our budget calculation, not an application of either theorem outside its hypotheses.

The newer paper uses an internal periodic-weight determinant-counting result as part of its proof. That may be a separate route to investigate, but its full level, variable-range and error ledger has not been attached here. Failure of the two printed main theorems does not rule out that adaptation.

## 7. The additional twisted-moment lead is conditional and below the length boundary

Baluyot and Turnage-Butterbaugh, author manuscript "Twisted 2kth moments of primitive Dirichlet L-functions: Beyond the diagonal", Theorem 2.2, PDF page 6: it assumes the GENERALIZED LINDELOF HYPOTHESIS and treats smooth Dirichlet-polynomial approximations of length X=Q^eta, 1<eta<2, with shifts O(1/log Q). Its weighted twists satisfy a range 0<vartheta<2-eta. [Primary author manuscript](https://sieg-baluyot.github.io/dirichlet.pdf)

Original A2022 supplies no GLH assumption. The actual unremoved natural coefficient scale T0 is larger than P^2, outside eta<2 for Q about P. The retained Mellin heights up to L^6 and the high carrier t_c are not O(1/log P) shifts. The same-character generalized-divisor coefficients also do not include the distinct chi psi factor automatically. Finite inverse twist coefficients might fit a sufficiently short admissible twist range, but that alone does not repair these independent gates. We do not use this conditional theorem or replace finite G by its infinite model.

## 8. Exact conclusion and next unpaid arithmetic requirement

No tested printed theorem provides the actual density expansion or weighted cumulative remainder of (1.1). The audit preserves the original d,e, both parity congruences, all primes, actual time carrier and Mellin exponents. It neither promotes an almost-all statement into a progression-uniform statement nor assumes its exceptional rows negligible.

A potentially useful next theorem would need explicit control of the finite-inverse, mixed chi/complex-shift four-factor correlations, including the h p sampling and reflected part, at the original high carrier height. Alternatively it could directly bound the aggregate weighted remainder without pointwise control. Those are new arithmetic statements, not consequences asserted from the references here.
